# Community standards

This project is intended to be a safe learning environment for students and educators.

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Do not publish personal information or student data.
- Do not use this project to bypass protections in commercial games or services.
- Welcome beginners and explain unfamiliar terms.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, and deliberate
privacy violations are not acceptable. Report conduct concerns privately to the
MeritPoint Academy repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community.
